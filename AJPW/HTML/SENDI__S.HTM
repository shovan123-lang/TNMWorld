SHOW WRITE-UP


Too Cold Scorpio vs. Billy Joe Travis


Winner: Too Cold Scorpio (Flying Scorpio Splash)Scorpio wasted no time 
electrifying the Sendai crowd, hitting the ring with speed and confidence.
Travis tried to slow the pace, but Scorpio’s athleticism was overwhelming. 
A crisp Flying Scorpio Splash ended things in just over two minutes, 
giving Scorpio a highlight-reel win and setting the tone for the night.



Akira Taue & Kenta Kobashi vs. The British Bulldogs


Winners: Taue & Kobashi (Kobashi pins Davey Boy with a flying legdrop)
A standout tag bout that blended the Bulldogs’ veteran precision with the 
youthful fire of Taue and Kobashi. Dynamite Kid brought his trademark intensity,
but Kobashi’s energy and Taue’s size advantage kept the Japanese duo in control.
The finish came when Kobashi soared with a flying legdrop to pin Davey Boy Smith,
earning a major victory over one of the world’s most respected teams.



Bobby Fulton vs. Rick Rude


Winner: Bobby Fulton (dropkick) With Johnny Ace a no-show, Rick Rude entered 
the match irritated and overconfident — and it cost him. Fulton weathered 
Rude’s power offense and capitalized on a rare mistake, catching Rude flush
with a dropkick for the upset pin. The Sendai crowd erupted at the shock finish,
while Rude stormed to the back furious.



Tommy Rogers & Masao Inoue vs. Richard Slinger & Tsuyoshi Kikuchi


Winners: Rogers & Inoue (Rogers submits Slinger with a sleeper)
Despite Yoshinari Ogawa’s absence, this turned into a crisp, competitive 
tag match. Kikuchi’s fiery offense kept the pace high, but Rogers’ technical
mastery ultimately dictated the finish. After isolating Slinger, 
Rogers cinched in a tight sleeperhold for the submission.
A strong showing for both teams and one of the night’s most complete matches.



Abdullah the Butcher, Giant Kimala II & Stan Hansen vs. Misawa,
Dory Funk Jr. & Masa Fuchi



Winners: Abdullah pins Misawa (back elbow)Chaos reigned as the monstrous
trio of Butcher, Kimala II, and Hansen overwhelmed their opponents with sheer
brutality. Misawa fought valiantly, trading stiff shots with Hansen and trying
to rally the crowd, but the numbers game proved too much. Abdullah caught Misawa
with a heavy back elbow for the pin, giving the foreign monsters a major victory
and adding heat to the ongoing Misawa-Hansen rivalry.



All Asia Tag Team Championship 


Miracle Violence Connection vs. The Can-Am Express


Result: Double Count-Out — Can-Am Express retain 
A hard-hitting, 23-minute war between two of the best teams on the planet.
Williams and Gordy brought their trademark smash-mouth style, while Furnas 
and Kroffat countered with speed, technique, and explosive power. 
The match spilled to the floor late, with all four men brawling until the 
referee counted both teams out. The crowd wanted a decisive finish, but the 
inconclusive ending only heightened anticipation for the inevitable rematch.



Overall Impression 

Night 3 of the Summer Action Series delivered a balanced,
engaging card with strong tag wrestling, a major upset, and heavyweight violence.
Kobashi’s continued rise, the Bulldogs’ strong showing, and the simmering 
tension between Misawa’s camp and the foreign giants gave the tour real 
momentum heading into its next stops.
