Summer Crush 91 Tour Night 3 | July 6th, 1991
Day 3: Osaka Japan|Venue: Osaka Prefectural Gym
Attendance: 5,000 AVG: Ticket $25.00
Gate: $125,000 Broadcast: TV Asahi (NJPW World Pro Wrestling)

Recap:
The biggest crowd of the tour so far packed into the Osaka Prefectural Gym
for the World Pro Wrestling taping, and the IWGP Heavyweight Champion had
something to prove. One night after Masahiro Chono rolled him up in
Niigata, Riki Choshu answered the challenge Chono set for him. Mike
Rotunda, the amateur specialist Chono hand-picked, took Choshu to the mat
early and worked the arm, but Choshu fought out and dropped him with the
Riki lariato at 15:08. The match never caught fire (** 1/2), but the
message was delivered. Choshu stood over Rotunda with the belt held high
and pointed toward the entrance way, where Chono was nowhere to be found.

Chono didn't need to be there. Earlier in the night he made Tony Halme tap
to the STF in just over seven minutes, and he never looked at the big Finn
after the bell. He went straight to the camera and held up the STF hand
signal. Sumo Hall is three weeks away.

One night after winning the IWGP Tag Team Championship, Shinya Hashimoto
lost his temper. Teaming with Kengo Kimura instead of Junji Hirata, he lost
by disqualification to Bam Bam Bigelow and Corporal Kirchner when he
refused to stop kicking Kirchner in the corner. Bigelow and Kirchner were
on opposite sides in Niigata, and nobody in the building expected them to
walk out together tonight.

The hidden gems were in the middle of the card. Kim Duk and Norio Honaga
outlasted Hiroshi Hase and Jeff Gaylord in a 27-minute war (**** 1/4), with
Honaga flying off the top with an elbow drop on Gaylord. Kim Duk has now
been on the winning side every night of the tour. Kuniaki Kobayashi and
Shiro Koshinaka matched it (**** 1/4) in eight minutes flat, with Koshinaka
power bombing Keith Hart for the pin.

Tatsumi Fujinami, Keiji Mutoh and El Samurai beat Scott Norton, Samu and
Brian Pillman when Samu was counted out. It was a good bounce-back for El
Samurai two nights after the Pegasus Kid wiped him out in 32 seconds at
Korakuen.

The opener lasted 70 seconds. Savio Vega was disqualified, giving Pegasus
Kid, Hiro Saito and Mando Guerrero the win over Jushin Liger, Kensuke
Sasaki and Vega. Afterward, NJPW officials made the announcement promised
in Niigata. Liger will defend the IWGP Junior Heavyweight Championship at
Sumo Hall on July 30th against the Pegasus Kid. Kid stared Liger down from
the apron, and Liger answered by pointing at the belt.

The card came in at ** 1/2, the lowest of the tour, despite two **** 1/4
matches. The building held 8,000 and drew 5,000, but the $125,000 gate is
the biggest of the tour so far. The TV show airs this week on TV Asahi.

Summer Crush heads to Nagoya on Wednesday!
