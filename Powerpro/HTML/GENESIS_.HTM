Nashville, TN (Fairgrounds)
May 10th, 1991
Attendance 425


The Music City will be the new home of the USWA wrestling Academy, "Power Pro"
Wrestling.

A few wrestling legends have come back to the territory to help run the 
performance center, and train the new talent. 

Power Pro will run 1 show a month from the fairgrounds and will have a new 
talent acquisition every 6 months. 

Ron Bass, Rocky Johnson, Buzz Sawyer are the inaugural trainers to be 
hired by USWA. 

We kicked off the first year of the Academy with Genesis! This show was
to feature a tournament for the Powwer Pro Championship, but that would end 
in a controversy when we had a no show, leading Modest to the FINALS without
the title being on the line. 

Rocky Johnson, who was named the head trainer, announced that a 3 way dance
will take place next month and we will crown the fist ever champion. 

In other action, Buzz Sawyer has taken an interest in the the Thrillseakers.
He attempted to convince them that he should manage them to the top of the USWA, 
but they passed on the offer. 

This led to a match with Lance Storm, in which the student out wrestled the 
teacher to win the match. 

Our next show at the Fiargrounds will take place on Sunday June 30th, 
when we introduce the first show in a series of events named "Level Up" 

The only match announcement tonight was Tony Devito, Mike Awesome and 
Mike Modest in a 3 way dance for the title.
