Match observations:

Viernes Espectacular | July 12th, 1991
Mexico City, MX | Venue: Arena Mexico
Attendance: 15,000 AVG: Ticket $13.50
Gate: $202,500 Tone: Major Show

The road to Verano de Escandalo is officially paved, and it runs straight
through the hair of Vampiro Canadiense. Arena Mexico was buzzing all night
as the Torneo de Tercias kicked off, a new masked hero arrived, and El
Dandy showed the world which side he is really on.

The night opened with the Casas brothers together for the first time.
Negro Casas and Felino were a step ahead of Jerry Estrada and Javier Cruz
from the opening bell, and Negro Casas took both falls with the Casita.
The family looked right at home on a Friday night.

All eyes were on the CMLL debut of The Lightning Kid, and the young
American did not disappoint. He flew around Arena Mexico like he had
been wrestling here for years, and the crowd was with him the whole way.
But Art Barr knew exactly how to ground him. Barr took the first fall
with the Frog Splash, then went after the Kid's leg and trapped him in a
figure-four for the second. The Kid lost tonight, but he won over a lot
of fans. We will be seeing a lot more of him.

The ring announcer then unveiled the bracket for the Torneo de Tercias:
eight trios, three weeks, and the first CMLL World Trios Championship on
the line at Verano de Escandalo.

In the first quarterfinal, Atlantis was the star once again, pinning
Masakre with the Atlantida and Jaque Mate with a flying cross body to
send Atlantis, Rayo de Jalisco Jr. and Angel Azteca to the semifinals.
Los Intocables did not take it well. They jumped Azteca after the bell
and put the boots to him until a masked man nobody had seen before hit
the ring and cleared it. It was OCTAGON, making his CMLL debut. He stood
in the middle of the ring, eyes locked on Masakre, and the two had to be
held apart. Something tells us Masakre will be seeing that mask again.

The second quarterfinal had the whole building talking before the bell.
Satanico and MS-1 walked out alone, refusing to name their third man.
Then the music changed and out came EL DANDY. The Light Heavyweight
Champion has joined Los Infernales! Dandy made it count early, cradling
Volador, one half of the National Tag Team Champions, for the first
fall. But Ultimo Dragon answered with a stunning inverted DDT on Dandy,
and El Hijo del Santo wrapped Satanico in the camel clutch to send the
tecnicos through. The Infernales are out of the tournament, but Dandy's
pin on a tag champion will not be forgotten.

Eddie Guerrero vs. Brazo de Plata was the match of the night. Eddie hit
the Frog Splash for the first fall. In the second, Brazo de Plata
looked to have him beat, but Chavo Guerrero climbed onto the apron and
pulled the referee's attention. By the time the official turned back,
Eddie was flying off the top with another Frog Splash. Fuerza Guerrera
came out afterward and got right in Chavo's face. The Guerrero family
does not look like one happy family tonight.

In the main event, Vampiro Canadiense and Kamala shocked Konnan and Blue
Panther. Vampiro caught Panther with a neck snap and pinned him clean.
Then Vampiro grabbed a pair of scissors and the microphone, held the
scissors up to his own hair, and challenged Konnan: his hair against
Konnan's Mexican National Heavyweight Championship at Verano de
Escandalo. Konnan never hesitated. He accepted. Before anyone could
celebrate, Cien Caras came storming down the aisle, shouting that HE
deserved the title shot. Vampiro shoved him, Caras shoved back, and the
locker room had to pull them apart.

July 26th: Konnan vs. Vampiro, Title vs. Hair. Plus the trios final.
Verano de Escandalo cannot come soon enough!

CMLL drew a strong crowd on 7/12 at Arena Mexico for the first night of
the Torneo de Tercias, which will crown the promotion's first World
Trios Champions at the Verano de Escandalo supercard on 7/26. The big
angle saw Vampiro, who pinned Blue Panther in the main event, challenge
Konnan to title vs. hair for Verano, with Cien Caras getting into a
shoving match with Vampiro over who deserves the shot. El Dandy was
revealed as the third member of Los Infernales and pinned tag champion
Volador, although the Infernales lost and are out of the tournament.
Octagon debuted with a save, and Eddie Guerrero vs. Brazo de Plata was
the best match on the show. The Lightning Kid's debut against Art Barr
was well received. In other news, Jushin Liger and The Blue Blazer are
booked for CMLL's first show in Acapulco this Wednesday. Liger's trip
reportedly came together through the New Japan office, which wants him
working with Mexican juniors before a possible late-tour return and is
said to be eyeing Ultimo Dragon for future junior tours. The Blazer, who
everyone knows is Owen Hart, is using the WWF's three days off between
WrestleFest and the 7/18 Richfield TV taping to work under the hood. One
source said Owen has been frustrated with being booked as "the little
brother" in the Heenan angle and jumped at the chance to wrestle a
fast-paced style again, though the WWF office is said to be less than
thrilled with him flying out of Mexico on the morning of a TV taping.
Mike Rotunda is also booked on the show against Atlantis.

Kevin Sullivan, who has not been seen on WCW television since Columbus,
was strongly rumored to be at Arena Mexico on Friday, and several
Mexican magazines went to press saying he would be in the building. He
never showed. According to people close to the situation, Sullivan flew
into Mexico City midweek to meet with the CMLL office about working the
Gran Prix in August and possibly helping with the foreign talent. The
meetings ran long and the two sides could not agree on dates, so CMLL
decided not to advertise him until a deal was done rather than risk
another Espectro Jr.-style no-show. Negotiations with CMLL are said to
be ongoing. With Sullivan's Dark Circle having been taken over by Cactus
Jack, there is a lot of speculation that he may be headed south of the
border for an extended run, but nothing has been signed.
