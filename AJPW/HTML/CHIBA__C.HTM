Match observations: 

Summer Action Series Tour | July 3rd,1991
Day 2: Chiba Japan|Venue: Chiba Port Arena
Attendance: 2,315 AVG: Ticket $31.00 
Gate: $71,765.00 Broadcast: N/A

In a major turning point for the tour, and a seismec moment in the history
of AJPW, tonight will forever be known as "The Fall of the Giant" Giant Baba, 
who had a firey speech two days ago at Korakuen Hall, is out indefinitely.
He was attacked during the match with a fork targeting the neck of the beloved 
Giant Baba. A recipt from Korakuen when Baba defeated Abdullah with a running
neckbreaker. 

Johnny Ace, who was in Baba's corner, pushed Abdullah off, and Joe Higuchi
called for the bell. Paramedics rushed to the ring as the fans looked on
in horror as Baba was stretchered out of the ring into a waiting ambulance.

With the locker room still reeling from the aftermath of Baba's injuries, a 
Triple Crown title match was still to take place. Jumbo Tsuruta would make
his first title defense of the tour, giving an opportunity to a Super
Generation faction member, and former student, Toshiaki Kawada. Kawada has
returned to All Japan after being with the USWA on excursion. His matches
against Bret Hart, Bill Dundee, and even Scotty Flamingo have tested his
skills and have excellerated is experience learning various syles of North
American Technical Wrestling. 

The match was a magnificent display of brutality and power, as Jumbo defended
his title with a devistating waist lock suplex, that set up the Jumbo knee.
The match had the fans on their feet. When Kawada stood up from the beating,
the crowd applauded in a show of respect for the young lion. 

Steve Williams and Gordy teamed with Stan Hansen in a match that pitted Dory
Funk Jr. up against an agressive rival, Stan Hansen. Hansen was relentless 
in this victory, pinning Dory Funk jr, after busting him open late in the 
match. Doc and Gordy are in the prime of their careers, and are tearing 
through the compeitition after completely dominating Taue and Ogawa. It 
seems like they wanted a little revenge for their loss on opening night. 

Joe and Dean Malenko have been established technical wrestlers in AJPW for 
quite some time. With a new breed of Jr. Heavyweights coming into their own,
Joe and Dean have announced that they will be the gate keepers of the division,
and if anyone wanted to test them, sign the contracts. Kikuchi was the first
Jr to take the challenge, and he fell to Joe in our opening match.
