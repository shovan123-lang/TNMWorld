Match observations: 

ALL STAR WRESTLING
Butlins, Bognor Regis — Friday 7th June 1991

A night of thrills, spills, and championship drama under the Butlins
lights! All Star Wrestling brought the very best of British and
international mat action to the south coast, and the fans were treated to
a night they won't soon forget!

---

MATCH 1 NUMBER ONE CONTENDERS MATCH FOR THE BRITISH HEAVYWEIGHT
CHAMPIONSHIP
Robbie Brookside defeated Tony St. Clair (11:33)
Winning move: Bridging German Suplex

Young Robbie Brookside put on a display of breathtaking technical
wrestling to open the evening in style! Tony St. Clair, the seasoned
veteran, gave the Liverpool lad everything he had — but Brookside's
lightning-quick instincts saw him bridge back with a stunning German
Suplex to steal the pin and earn himself a future shot at Giant Haystacks
and the British Heavyweight Championship! The crowd erupted as the
youngster celebrated — this boy has a big future ahead of him!

PROMOTER'S NOTE: A strong opener that did exactly what we needed —
Brookside is over with the fans and this result sets up a compelling
future title challenge. St. Clair remains credible in defeat; his
experience kept this match grounded and gave Robbie something to work
against. Internally — Brookside's push continues on schedule.

---

MATCH 2
Bob Backlund defeated Doc Dean (29:20)
Winning move: Fisherman Suplex

In what was undoubtedly the match of the evening, the former World
Champion Bob Backlund and the gutsy Doc Dean went to war for nearly
thirty minutes in a breathtaking display of pure wrestling! The Butlins
crowd were on their feet throughout as Dean — game as ever — refused to
stay down. But Backlund's class and conditioning ultimately proved the
difference, hooking the leg beautifully with the Fisherman Suplex to take
the three count. A standing ovation was richly deserved by both men!

PROMOTER'S NOTE: An absolute showstopper. Backlund is in tremendous shape
and the crowd responded to every near-fall. Nearly thirty minutes and
they never lost the audience — that is a testament to both men's
professionalism. Doc Dean should be proud of his performance tonight.
Internally — consider building Dean further on the back of this
performance. He more than held his own with a former World Champion.

---

MATCH 3  TAG TEAM MATCH
Ivan Putski & Danny Boy Collins defeated Dave Taylor & Fit Finlay (13:22)
Winning move: Ivan Putski pinned Dave Taylor with the Polish Hammer

The Butlins ballroom shook when Ivan Putski unleashed the Polish Hammer
on Dave Taylor! Putski and young Danny Boy Collins made for an unlikely
but effective partnership, and the crowd loved every moment of it. Taylor
and Finlay — one of the most dangerous and dirty duos in All Star
Wrestling today — used every trick in the book to keep Collins grounded,
but when Putski got the hot tag, it was all over. BOOM — Polish Hammer —
goodnight Dave Taylor!

PROMOTER'S NOTE: Good, crowd-pleasing tag action. Putski is a massive
draw with the holidaymakers — they know who he is and they pop huge for
the Polish Hammer. Finlay and Taylor are excellent heels and made the
babyfaces look great. Internally — Taylor and Finlay's chemistry as a
team is developing nicely. Worth continuing to book them together.

---

MATCH 4
Steve Regal defeated Iron Mike Sharpe (10:14)
Winning move: The Regal Stretch (submission)

Steve Regal was in a foul mood tonight and Iron Mike Sharpe paid the
price. The self-proclaimed "Man's Man" dominated proceedings with a cold,
calculated and thoroughly unpleasant performance — wearing Sharpe down
before locking in the devastating Regal Stretch. Sharpe had nowhere to go
and was forced to tap. Regal stood over his fallen opponent with a look
of utter contempt. Charming as ever.

PROMOTER'S NOTE: Regal's character work continues to be top notch — the
crowd genuinely despise him, which is exactly what we want. This was a
short and effective showcase. Sharpe is a reliable hand for this kind of
match. Internally — Regal needs a meaningful feud soon. His work is too
good to be wasted on showcase matches indefinitely.

---

MATCH 5
Bruce Hart defeated Hector Guerrero (11:14)
Winning move: Running Neckbreaker Drop

Two internationally acclaimed grapplers locked horns in a spirited
contest that gave the Bognor faithful a taste of the global game! Hector
Guerrero — with that famous fighting spirit in his blood — pushed Hart
hard, but the son of Stu Hart showed tremendous ring savvy, catching
Guerrero off the ropes and planting him with the Running Neckbreaker Drop
for the victory. A solid and entertaining bout!

PROMOTER'S NOTE: Good international flavour to this match — both men are
polished performers and it showed. The crowd appreciated the quality even
if they weren't fully invested in either man. Internally — a useful match
to have on the card but neither man is currently driving ticket sales on
their own in this territory.

---

MAIN EVENT — BRITISH HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP
Giant Haystacks defeated Johnny Saint (via countout, 16:00)
Giant Haystacks RETAINS the British Heavyweight Championship

The champion came. The champion saw. The champion retained — but not in
the fashion he would have hoped! The monstrous Giant Haystacks, defending
the British Heavyweight Championship, found himself in deeper water than
expected against the wily and beloved Johnny Saint. Saint's ring craft
had Haystacks tied in knots at times — the crowd were beside themselves
with excitement! But when the action spilled to the outside, Saint could
not beat the referee's count, and Haystacks retained his title on a
countout. The champion's belt stays put — but Saint was robbed of the
victory he deserved and the fans let Haystacks know it in no uncertain
terms!

PROMOTER'S NOTE: The countout finish protects Saint strongly — he was
never beaten and the crowd know it. Haystacks' heat as champion is
running well; the fans want to see him beaten legitimately. Saint is
arguably the most beloved performer on the roster and this result leaves
the door open. Internally — a Saint rematch with a proper finish should
be considered. There is money in this feud if booked correctly.
