ALL STAR WRESTLING - HOLIDAY CAMP SPECTACULAR

Camber Sands, Pontins | Segment 1 (3:00)

The house lights come up on the Pontins ballroom. Every seat is taken, and
the campers at the back are standing on their chairs. Brian Dixon climbs
through the ropes in a blazer and tie, takes the microphone from the ring
announcer and waits for the noise to settle.

BRIAN DIXON: Good evening, Camber Sands!

The crowd roars back at him.

BRIAN DIXON: Now then. Hands up who's been here all week. Hands up who only
got here Saturday. And hands up who's spent the whole day on that beach and
is going to be very red in the morning.

Laughter around the ballroom. Dixon points at a sunburnt man in the front
row and shakes his head.

BRIAN DIXON: Ladies and gentlemen, boys and girls, welcome to All Star
Wrestling. I promised you a summer you'd never forget, and tonight we're
doing something we haven't done on this tour yet. Tonight, the British
Heavyweight Championship is on the line.

A big cheer, then boos as the name of the champion sinks in.

BRIAN DIXON: Last Saturday at Bognor Regis, Giant Haystacks kept his title
against Johnny Saint. He didn't beat him. He got counted out of trouble,
and everyone in that building knew it.

Loud boos for Haystacks.

BRIAN DIXON: The same night, a young man from Liverpool beat Tony St. Clair
to become the number one contender. So tonight, in our main event, for the
British Heavyweight Championship, it's Giant Haystacks against Robbie
Brookside!

The ballroom erupts. A group of children near the aisle start chanting for
Robbie.

BRIAN DIXON: I've told the champion, and I'll tell you. There'll be no
hiding on the outside tonight. If Robbie Brookside can beat him, he goes
home with that belt.

BRIAN DIXON: But that's not all. The former WWF Champion, Bob Backlund,
hasn't lost on this tour, and he says he'll wrestle any man in Britain.
Tonight the first man to take him up on it is the Belfast Bruiser himself,
Fit Finlay.

Cheers for Backlund, and a smattering of boos for Finlay.

BRIAN DIXON: Johnny Saint, who was robbed at Bognor, takes on Steve Regal.
Gary Albright meets the Polish Hammer, Ivan Putski. And to start us off,
Liverpool's own Doc Dean is in action against Skull Murphy.

Dixon lowers the microphone for a moment, then lifts it again with a small
smile.

BRIAN DIXON: And keep your eyes open tonight, because I'm told there may be
one or two surprises before the night is out. I'm not saying any more than
that.

A murmur runs round the room. Somebody at the back shouts the name of a
very big man in a top hat. Dixon pretends not to hear it.

BRIAN DIXON: Enjoy the show, Camber Sands. Let's have some wrestling!

Dixon hands the microphone back to the ring announcer and climbs out to a
big round of applause as Doc Dean's music starts.
