## How it's counted
- **WWF and WCW (monthly salary):** salary accrues day by day through the world date (monthly rate × days ÷ 31). Tom Zenk's starts 7/11. Rates from `claude/TNM - World Talent Pay Scales (All Promotions).md`.
- **USWA, CMLL, ASW, Tri-State, Power Pro (per appearance):** base rate × the show's multiplier, plus the main-event and semi-main gate cuts split evenly, for every show that has run (including Tri-State 7/19 and Power Pro 7/28, which have already run ahead of the world date). Local enhancement talent not on a scale (Johnny Rich, Jan Vang, Jody Burt, Jos LeDuc, Kerry Brown) get $15 a TV appearance.
- **AJPW and NJPW (per tour):** the full tour fee shows as **due** until the last night (AJPW 7/27, NJPW 7/30), then moves to paid. Part-timers will be prorated when the tour closes. **Exception: Toshiaki Kawada** (leaving after Okayama 7/15) is paid per appearance instead, $1,000 a night (his $14,000 fee ÷ 14 nights), counted as each night runs (Sean, 2026-11-11). 5 nights so far = $5,000; Okayama makes it $6,000.
- **Bonuses:** added on the night. So far: WrestleFest '91 main-event bonus, $25,678.50 each to Hogan and Slaughter. The Bash main-event bonus goes in once the Bash is logged.
- **Not included:** announcers; Kevin Sullivan (worked three WCW dates before quitting 7/8, isn't on the pay scale).
- The same wrestler is combined across promotions (e.g. Sting in WCW and NJPW, Owen Hart as the Blue Blazer, IRS as Mike Rotunda, Eddie Guerrero and Santo in USWA and CMLL).
