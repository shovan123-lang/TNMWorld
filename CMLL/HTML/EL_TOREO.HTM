Match observations: 

Lucha Libre en Nacalpan| July 10th, 1991
Nacalpan, MX | Venue: El Toreo
Attendance: 6,000 AVG: Ticket $10.80 
Gate: $64,800 Tone: Midweek Showcase

The Debut of EMLL is Nacalpan was a hit, bringing in a crowd of over 6,000
to the El Toreo bull riding stadium. 

Summer sparks fly in El Toreno tonight with CMLL coming to the venue for the
first time.

Our six man feature started the show. El Hijo del Santo was nothing short
of magnificent, submitting MS-1 with a Camel Clutch in the first fall and
then forcing Masakre to surrender to a fujiwara armbar in the second. With
Negro Casas and Angel Azteca backing him, the tecnicos swept the
rudos 2-0 and had the fans chanting Santo's name to warm them up for the rest
of the evening.

La Diabolica continued her summer surge, spinning Lola Gonzalez into the mat
with the Diabolica Spin for a decisive Victory. The womens division has
been heating up, and Diabolica is making a strong case for a title shot.

In a heated Captain's rules bout, Volador and Rayo de Jalisco Jr. 
outmaneuvered Silver King and Satanico, with Volador soaring through the air
to crush Silver King with a moonsault Bodyblock. The tecnicos' chemistry was
undeniable, and the rudos' frustration was palable. 

Atlantis scored a clean win over Black Magic, catching him offguard with a 
sharp slap that sutnned the foreigner long enough for the pin. The rivalry
between Atlantis and the foreign contingent continues to simmer.

And in the nights most intense singles battle, Konnan muscled past Apolo
Dantes planting him with a spinebuster slam that shook the ring. The fans
erupted-Konnan's star power is undeniable, and this was one of his strongest
Arena Coliseo outings of the year. 

Post match Konnan took the mic and thanked all the fans in attendance. He
said that CMLL will be back on a regular schedule because of the fantastic
crowd.

Backstage Notes:
6,000 in attendance was a strong Wednesday number, Konnan on top, along with
the debut show are the main reasons. We expect that number to drop a little
in the fall when kids go back to school, and the shine of a debut show wears off.

The $64k gate exceeded projections; walk-up was boosted by vacation season 
and EL Hijo del Santo being promoted. 

- La Diabolica is getting over more than expected; fans reacted big to 
her finish. The promotion is looking to bring in some ladies from All Japan 
Women to boost the womens divisiton.
