Match observations: 

Viernes Espectacular| July 5th, 1991
Mexico City, MX | Venue: Arena Mexico
Attendance: 14,000 AVG: Ticket $13.50 
Gate: $189,000 Tone: Major Show

As the summer of 91 rolls on, there is a lot going on south of the border. 
Legacies vs the Uprising, an invasion of international stars, and Family 
Warfare explodes around the territory. 

MS-1, Satanico, Jaque Mate and Cien Caras are all looking to dominate CMLL, 
and a potential new stable could arise from this combination. Lets take a look
at the happenings from Viernes Espectacular that kicked off July.

Heavyweight champion Konnan has been stalked by the unorthodox psychological
games of Vampiro Canadienese. Tonight, he had help from his friends. Atlantis,
and Rayo De Jalisco jr. teamed up with the champion to take on Vampiro,
Cien Caras, and Universo 2000.

Atlantis was the all star of this one, pinning both Cien Caras and Universo 2000
to get the victory for their team. Vampiro had a miscue with Cien Caras 
leading to the pinfall. The two Rudos had to be seperated on the way back
to the dressing room. 

In the semi main event, a mexican death match took place between Kamala
and Blue Panther. With Black Magic at ringside with Kamala. These two have
been battling all throughout the year, but it was tonight that Panther
challenged Kamala, and internally challenged himself to go toe to toe 
in a no timelimit mexican death match. A fight without honor that would
end right past the one hour mark. Blue Panther slayed the Monster and had his
hand held high. 

Chavo and his nephew Eddie agreed to team with Fuerza Guerrera to take
on Los Intocables tonight. Fuerza picked up both pinfalls, but Chavo was
none to happy with him getting all the attention in the match. Young
Eddie was beyond happy that they picked up the win. 

The Mexican National tag titles were on the line tonight and Volador and
El Hijo Del Santo were able to beat MS-1 and Satanico. The fans were on
their feet to watch this one. The two technios are performing at the 
best level of their respective careers. 

Ultimo Dragon, one of our international competitors
opened the show telling the audience He has brought one of the best female
wrestlers on the planet to arena mexico. Because CMLL doesn't have any
real female competition, Ultimo challenged her in an exibition because he
wants all the fans of CMLL to see her talent. 

The two had a back and forth affair, but it was Toshiyo Yamada picking up
a win over her training partner Ultimo Dragon. Yamada was showing respect
for the fans and in walks La Diabolica. This ruthless, agressive Ruda just
made Yamada aware that she is in her house now! 

The energy in the arena tonight was electric. Friday events are always must see!
CMLL will be bringing in some international talent from the USWA. Some of the
names being floated around are Scott Taylor, Rex King, Jimmy Valient and
The Moondogs.
