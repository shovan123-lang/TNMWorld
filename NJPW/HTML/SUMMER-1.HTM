Summer Crush 91 Tour Night 2 | July 5th, 1991

Masahiro Chono defeated Choshu with a rollup, putting self doubt in the head
of the IWGP Heavyweight Champion. This was the first time Choshu has been
defeated via pinfall on the tour, and it has everyone wondering if Chono is
going to be our next champion. After the match Chono addressed the crowd and
announced that Rotunda, an amateur wrestling specialist, will be taking on
Choshu in a singles bout in Osaka. Osaka's show will be taped to air on 
NJPW World Pro Wrestling this week. 

In other action, Bam Bam Bigelows losing streak didn't last long, with a
victory for himself and Mutoh over Inoki and Kirchner. Inoki checked on
his partner after the match and had a stare down with Mutoh as trainers came
to check on Kirshner. 

Liger defended his Jr. title over El Samurai. Tomorrow in Osaka, NJPW 
officials will announce Ligers opponent at Sumo Hall.
