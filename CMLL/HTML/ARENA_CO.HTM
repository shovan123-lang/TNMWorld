Family Warefare was tonights main event, with the Guerreros defending
their name against the Brazos! There is an undertone of family pride when
these two teams match up. Eddie and Chavo send the crowd home happy
in our main event. Brazo de Plata has challenged Eddie Guerrero for 
Friday night in Arena Mexico!

Our opener had the debut of La Diabolica to battle the youth and excitement
of Toshiyo Yamada. Ultimo Dragon went one on one with his student on Friday
night, but tonight we saw a female challenger step up. 

Konnan, Rayo De Jalasico jr, and Atlantis took on Vampiro and his foreign
alliance of Black Magic and Kamala. The match ended with Black Magic
getting DQed for throwing Atlantis over the top. A rematch in Puebla, this
time 2/3 falls, seems to be likely. 

Match of the night went to El Dandy for the sucessful defense of the National
Lightweight title. A fantasic match with Blue Panther. Panther held his hand
out for a handshake, but the champion El Dandy walked out of him in the ring.
The fans began to throw debris on the field.
