Mexico City, Arena Mexico | Segment 19 (3:00)

The ring is still a mess. Vampiro Canadiense is gone, but his scissors
lie in the middle of the canvas where he dropped them. The ring
announcer steps over them, picks them up, and holds them high. The
crowd boos.

RING ANNOUNCER: Damas y caballeros, you heard it with your own ears.
Vampiro Canadiense has put his hair on the line. And our champion,
Konnan, has accepted!

A huge roar. "Kon-nan! Kon-nan!" rolls around the building.

RING ANNOUNCER: Friday, July 26th, at Verano de Escandalo. For the
Mexican National Heavyweight Championship... Konnan's title against the
hair of Vampiro! Cabellera contra campeonato! If Vampiro loses, he will
be shaved bald, right here in the center of this ring!

He sets the scissors down on the turnbuckle pad and lets the crowd
react.

RING ANNOUNCER: And the Torneo de Tercias is on its way! Tonight,
Atlantis, Rayo de Jalisco Jr. and Angel Azteca advanced to the
semifinals. And Los Infernales, with their new partner, El Dandy, are
moving on as well!

Heavy boos at Dandy's name. Somebody in the upper deck throws a cup
toward the ring.

RING ANNOUNCER: The action rolls on this week! Tomorrow night, Saturday,
at Arena Coliseo: the Guerreros against La Legion Extranjera, and Los
Brazos against Los Hermanos Dinamita, to decide the final two
semifinalists!

RING ANNOUNCER: Sunday, in Guadalajara, Rayo de Jalisco Jr. comes home!
Rayo, Atlantis and Konnan against Los Hermanos Dinamita. And Blue
Panther is looking for El Dandy, wherever he is hiding!

RING ANNOUNCER: And on Wednesday, for the very first time, the CMLL
comes to Acapulco! An international night: from Japan, Jushin Liger!
With him, the masked Blue Blazer! They face Ultimo Dragon and The
Lightning Kid. Plus Atlantis against the American, Mike Rotunda! And in
the main event, Konnan against Cien Caras!

The crowd buzzes at the international names.

RING ANNOUNCER: Then next Friday, July 19th, right here, the semifinals
of the Torneo de Tercias. And two weeks from tonight, Verano de
Escandalo! The title. The hair. The trios crowns. You do not want to
miss it!

RING ANNOUNCER: Gracias, Arena Mexico! Buenas noches!

The house lights come up. The cameras catch one last shot of
Vampiro's scissors still hanging on the turnbuckle as the crowd files
out.
