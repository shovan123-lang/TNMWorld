Summer Crush 91 Tour Night 4 | July 10th, 1991
Day 4: Nagoya, Aichi Japan|Venue: Aichi Prefectural Gym
Attendance: 4,000 AVG: Ticket $25.00
Gate: $100,000 Broadcast: N/A

Recap:
After three days off, Summer Crush returned in Nagoya, and the Samoans made
the loudest statement of the tour. Samu and Kokina needed just 2:26 to wipe
out the team of IWGP Heavyweight Champion Riki Choshu and Hiro Saito.
Kokina flattened Saito with the double splash for the pin while Choshu was
still being held on the floor. Afa was in Niigata on Friday, and the family
has now made its presence felt twice. Choshu was helped to the back holding
his ribs, and New Japan officials have some questions to answer before Sumo
Hall.

It was a long night for Hiro Saito. He pulled double duty, taking the fall
for the champion in the Samoan squash after teaming with Tarzan Goto and
Scott Norton against Hase, Sasaki and Jushin Liger. Liger finished Goto
with the moonsault in that one, and Hase and Sasaki got back into the win
column after losing the tag titles in Niigata.

In the main event, Masahiro Chono, Keiji Mutoh and Shinya Hashimoto beat
Bam Bam Bigelow, Hiroyoshi Tenzan and Ken Patera when Hashimoto caught
Patera in a crucifix at 21:02 (*** 3/4). Mutoh and Chono were billed as a
team for the first time on the tour. While Choshu was getting run over by
the Samoans, Chono was standing tall in the main event.

Tiger Jeet Singh and Kim Duk beat Tatsumi Fujinami and Shiro Koshinaka
(*** 1/2) when Koshinaka passed out in the Tiger Claw. Singh kept the claw
on after the bell until young lions dragged him off. Kim Duk is now four
for four on the tour.

The Pegasus Kid's road to Sumo Hall hit a bump in the opener. Osamu Kido
and Kengo Kimura beat the Kid and The Blue Blazer in a 35-minute match
(****), the best of the night, when Kido took Kid over with a side suplex
for the pin. Kid sat in the ring for a long time after, and Liger watched
from the entrance way.

Kuniaki Kobayashi pinned Tony Halme with the fisherman suplex. Halme has
lost three straight, and the crowd started to turn on him.

The card came in at ***, back up from Osaka. The Aichi Prefectural Gym drew
4,000 of its 7,500 seats for a $100,000 gate, the second-biggest of the
tour behind Osaka.
