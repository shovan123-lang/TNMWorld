Match observations: 

Summer Action Series Tour | July 9th,1991
Day 5:  |Venue: Kanazawa, Ishikawa Gymn
Attendance: 3,5000 AVG: Ticket $25.00 
Gate: $87,500 Broadcast: Highlights for Nippon TV.

AJPW returned to Kanazawa with a card that delivered surprises,breakout
performances, and one of the most shocking main events of the entire Summer
Action Series tour. With Giant Baba still absent following 
Abdullah the Butcher's heinous attack, the roster fought with renewed urgency- 
and in the main event,a new threat to the Triple Crown emerged. 

Eigen's tough night opened the show
Harika Eigen vs Masao Inoue
Eigen's comedy stylings got the crowd warmed up, but the tone shifted when
he came up clutching his elbow. Even in victory, the veteran limped to the back,
leaving fans wondering how this will affect the undercard dynamics for the
rest of the tour. 

Fantastics shock the world
The Fantastics vs Spivey and Stan Hansen
Spivey and Hansen are back in Japan to join the tour, and on their first night
back, in a stunning upset, the Fantastics finally broke their losing streak-
and they did it against two of the most dangerous men in the company. Rodgers 
caught Spivey in a tight roll-up, and the crowd erupted. Hansen stormed around
ringside afterward, furious, while the Fantastics celebrated like they'd won 
the World Tag Titles. This is the kind of win that can change a team's entire 
tour.

Johnny Ace vs Too Cold Scorpio
This was Ace's best performance of the tour so far. Scorpio pushed the pace,
but Ace hung with him step-for-step and finally nailed the Implant DDT for a
clean, decisive victory. The crowd gave Ace a strong ovation- he's clearly 
stepping into the "young foreign babyface" role with confidence. This was
Too Colds first loss since arriving in AJPW. 

Can-Am Express vs Kenta Kobashi & Akira Taue.
The All Asian tag titles were on the line in this featured match. It was the
match of the night until the main event. Kobashi was electric, Taue was intense,
and Can-Am were flawless. The finishing stretch was pure All Japan
magic-reversals,bombs, near-falls- until Kroffat finally planted Taue
with the Tiger Driver. Kobashi looked devestated afterward, but the crowd 
gave all four men a standing ovation. 

Misawa & British Bulldogs vs Rick Rude Abdullah the Butcher & Giant Kimala II 
Miswaw got his hands on Abdullah again- and the crowd was molten for every 
second of it. The Bulldogs brought their signature speed and stiffness, while
Rude and Abby leaned into chaos. Misawa finishing Kimala II clean sent a 
message: He's coming to Budokan for blood! Abdullah laughed as he retreated up
the aisle, fork in hand. 

Steve Williams vs Kawada
This was the moment the entire tour shifted. 
Kawada fought with everything he had- Kicks,suplexes, elbows- but Williams
absorbed it all. The finish was emphatic: Williams caught Kawada on a
charge, muscled him up, and ran him down with the Oklahoma Stampede.
1-2-3.
Clean.
Dominant.
Undeniable. 

As Williams stood over Kawada, the crowd buzzed with a realization:
This man might be the next Triple Crown Champion! 
After the match, Williams grabbed the microphone-something he rarley does.
"Jumbo...you're the champion. 
but I'm the strongest wrestler in this company.
you're next."

Jumbo Tsuruta walked to the ring, silent,and unblinking. Two men stood
forehead to forehead as the crowd roared. 

A new challenger has arrived! 

Backstage Notes:
Williams has become the clear Budokan challenger. The win over Kawada
gave him instant credibility. The post match confrontation with Jumbo
was a big hit with the crowd. Highlights will air on TV this week.

The feud between Misawa and Abby is the hottest thing going this summer. 
So much emotion is in this story with Baba on the shelf. Baba wants to
book this as a texas death match to take it over the top to headline
the Budokan show. 

We learned in the post show press conference that Eigen's elbow injury was
not as serious as first expected. He will miss shows in Nagoya,
Hiroshima and Okayama, but should rejoin the tour when we get to Kumamoto.
