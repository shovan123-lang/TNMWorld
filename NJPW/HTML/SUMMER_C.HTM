Day 1: Tokyo Japan|Venue: Korakuen Hall
Attendance: 1,750 AVG: Ticket $32.00 
Gate: $56,000 Broadcast: TV Asahi

Recap:
New Japans Summer Crush tour kicked off from the world famous Korakuen Hall. 
The building is a staple of Japanese wrestling with it's hardcore wrestling
fans, it's the perfect place to kick off a new tour! 

The IWGP Champion Riki Choshu, and Bam Bam Bigelow put on a show for the 
crowd in a NJPW Classic title match. The fans in attendance were hanging
from the bleachers as this main event reached it's climax. Bam Bam Bigelow 
has officially signed on with NJPW and immediately becomes a threat to
the title, but tonight Riki defended his belt to kick off what is likely to
be the biggest tour of the summer! 

The post match saw Riki take the mic, thank the fans for joining them tonight,
and a promised that Summer Crush 91 will be an historic tour, leading to
the first ever G1 Climax taking place in August. Riki says that defending
the title night in and night out cements his legacy, and that will be the
case this summer. He goes on to call out his opponent for Sumo Hall in
Masahiro Chono. The ooo's and aaahh of the crowd eventually lead to 
loud applause to end the night.

The rest of the evening showcased a variety of Jr. matches with tag
action between Mil Mascaras, The Blue Blazer and Keith Hart taking on
Tony Halme, Nogami, and Brian Pillman. World Championship Wrestling and 
NJPW have started a working relationship and Pillman will be here for the
July tour. 

Young lion Chris Benoit absolutely killed El Samurai in singles competiton.
Less than a minute. Trainers came out to check on El Samurai after he took a
devistationg Dragon Suplex. Word is that he is okay.

Jushin Liger defeated Mando Guerrero. After the match, Gran Hamada came out
for a confrontation from the enterance way. 

Summer Crush rolls on tomorrow night from Niigata!
