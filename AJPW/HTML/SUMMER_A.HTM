Day 1: Tokyo Japan|Venue: Korakuen Hall
Attendance: 1,750 AVG: Ticket $29.71 
Gate: $51,992 Broadcast: Nippon TV 

Recap:

This years Summer Action Series tour kicked off at Korakuen hall in front of
a packed house! The climate around AJPW is the rise of a new Generation, or
as they call themselves, The Super Generation. Kawada, back from a US excursion,
Kenta Kobashi, and Misawa challenged the old guard Tsuruta, Taue, and Eigen.

Although the Super Generation were unsuccessful in defeating the champion and
his partners, they went the distance and earned the respect of the champion. 
A generational clash is brewing in the wrestling rings of AJPW. 

The rules went out the window when Giant Baba stepped up to the challenge of
Abdullah the Butcher in a match without honor. Baba played Abby's game and 
came out the victor. Baba took the mic after the match and claimed that 
a new generation of stars would like him to move aside and retire, but
tonight he claims he will not go quietly into the night. Only he will 
decide his fate! The fans in Korakuen Hall burst into applause and chanted
"Baba, Baba" as they watched their hero.

Tag Team Turmoil kicked off the tour with multiple teams vying for supremacy. 
The Bulldogs looked fantastic as they took down Miracle Violence Connection
in a dream tag match. In a technical masterpiece, The Can Am Express defeated
The Fantastics. Misawa and Kawada are going to have their hands full when a
challenger for the tag titles is announced. 


Tour Notes:
Dan Spivey and Stan Hanson are scheduled to join the tour on July 7th in 
Niigata. They are in Dallas, TX working for the USWA's big Star Wars event.

It was announced to the live crowd that the finals of the tour will take place
on July 27th at Budokan Hall. The main event will be Misawa challenging the
Triple Crown champion Jumbo Tsuruta one on one!
