TORNEO DE TERCIAS: THE BRACKET

Mexico City, Arena Mexico | Segment 6 (3:00)

The house lights drop to half. A spotlight finds the ring announcer at
center ring as the big board above the entrance flickers to life with
eight trios and an empty space at the top marked "CAMPEONES MUNDIALES."

RING ANNOUNCER: Damas y caballeros! Ladies and gentlemen! Tonight, Arena
Mexico makes history. The Consejo Mundial de
Lucha Libre will crown the CMLL World Trios Champions!

The crowd roars. The announcer lets it build, then points up at the
board.

RING ANNOUNCER: Eight of the finest trios in the world. Three weeks.
One set of championships. This is the Torneo de Tercias!

The first two pairings light up on the left side of the board.

RING ANNOUNCER: Here in the first quarterfinal, tonight, the tecnicos
Atlantis, Rayo de Jalisco Jr. and Angel Azteca take on Los Intocables:
Jaque Mate, Masakre and Pierroth Jr.!

Boos for the Intocables, cheers for Atlantis and Rayo. A few fans near
ringside chant "Rayo! Rayo!"

RING ANNOUNCER: In the second quarterfinal, also tonight, the National
Tag Team Champions, El Hijo del Santo and Volador, join Ultimo Dragon
against Los Infernales: Satanico, MS-1 and a third partner they have
refused to name!

A murmur through the building. The board shows Satanico, MS-1 and a
black box with a question mark in it.

RING ANNOUNCER: Who stands with Los Infernales? We will find out
tonight!

The right side of the board lights up.

RING ANNOUNCER: Tomorrow night, Saturday, at Arena Coliseo, the
quarterfinals continue. Eddie Guerrero, Chavo Guerrero and Fuerza
Guerrera face La Legion Extranjera: Kamala, Black Magic and Art Barr!
And Los Brazos, Brazo de Oro, Brazo de Plata and El Brazo, against Los
Hermanos Dinamita: Cien Caras, Mascara Ano 2000 and Universo 2000!

The Brazos get the biggest pop of the segment. The Dinamitas get heavy
heat.

RING ANNOUNCER: The four winning trios advance to the semifinals, next
Friday, July 19th, right here at Arena Mexico. And the final will be
fought on Friday, July 26th, at the biggest show of the summer: Verano
de Escandalo!

The board fills in the semifinal and final lines, with the empty space
for the champions glowing at the top.

RING ANNOUNCER: Six men will leave Verano de Escandalo as the first
CMLL World Trios Champions. Who will it be? The road begins... right
now!

The lights come up and Los Intocables' music hits. Jaque Mate, Masakre
and Pierroth Jr. come through the curtain to a chorus of boos as the
board fades back to the CMLL logo.
